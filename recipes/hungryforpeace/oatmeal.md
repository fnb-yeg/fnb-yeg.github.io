## For 6

Ingredients:

- **2L** Water
- **2 tbsp** vanilla
- **&frac14; cup** Maple syrup, molasses, dark agave nectar, bananas, raisins, or apple cider
- **3 tbsp** sea salt (optional)
- **4 cups** rolled oats

Other Options:

- **&frac12; cups** raisins or chopped apples
- **&frac18; cups** shredded coconut
- **1 tbsp** nutmeg

Equipment:

- **2L** cooking pot

Bring water to a boil in a large pot. Add remaining ingrediants, return to a
boil, then turn to low heat. Stir often. Cook for two to five minutes. Remove
from heat. You can serve with margarine and sweetener or substitue bananas or
apple juice to sweeten the oatmeal.

## For 100

Ingredients:

- **11L** Water
- **1 cup** vanilla
- **1 cup** Maple syrup, molasses, dark agave nectar, bananas, raisins, or apple cider
- **2 tbsp** sea salt (optional)
- **50 cups** rolled oats

Other Options:

- **10 cups** raisins or chopped apples
- **8 cups** shredded coconut
- **4 tbsp** nutmeg

Equipment:

- **24L** cooking pot

Bring water to a boil in a large pot. Add remaining ingrediants, return to a
boil, then turn to low heat. Stir often. Cook for two to five minutes. Remove
from heat. You can serve with margarine and sweetener or substitue bananas or
apple juice to sweeten the oatmeal.
