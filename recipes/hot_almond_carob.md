A beverage with a rich mocha flavour. Ingredients:

- **4 cups** almond milk or almond beverage
- **1 tbsp** pure maple syrup
- **5 tsp** carob powder
- **5 tsp** roasted grain beverage powder

Equipment:

- Blender

Put all ingredients into a blender and blend until smooth. Pour into a saucepan
and warm gently over low heat. Do not boil; the beverage will separate. Serve.
