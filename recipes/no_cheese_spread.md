Ingredients:

- **5 tbsp** olive oil
- **1** small red bell pepper, seeded and coarsely chopped
- **1 &frac12; cups** raw cashews
- **&frac14; cup** raw sesame seeds
- **&frac12; cup** water
- **&frac13; cup** nutritional yeast flakes
- **&frac14; cup** lemon juice
- **&frac14; cup** canola or safflower oil
- **1 tbsp** Bragg Liquid Aminos (similar to soy sauce; used as a salt replacer)

Heat 1 tbsp of olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add bell pepper
and cook until tender; 5-7 min. Transfer to a blender

Add remaining ingredients and blend until smooth. Keeps up to a week in the
fridge.
