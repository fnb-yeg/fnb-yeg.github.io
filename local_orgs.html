Links to Local Organizations

Local groups whose work we love 💚

    Bear Claw Beaver Hills House
    Famous for serving the unhoused community with food, laughter and de-escalation tactics, BCP is a Canada-wide Indigenous-led movement.
    4B Harm Reduction
    An organisation made up of healtchare professionals and recovering substance users, who use their combined knowledge and experience to support people experiencing substance addiction in Edmonton. 4B hands out harm reduction supplies and regularly responds to overdoses
    Water Warriors
    An outreach organisation with many Indigenous members that goes on mobile outreach visits throughout the city, providing food and beverages, as well as harm reduction supplies, and doing checkups on community members to make sure they are safe
    New Grocery Movement
    One of our partners at one of our two community gardens this year, New Grocery Movement is active in food security work and believes in the right of everyone to have access to enough food, as well as in food sovereignty
