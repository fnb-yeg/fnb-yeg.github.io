## About Us

<p class="lead">With over a billion people going hungry each day how can we spend another dollar on war?</p>

Food Not Bombs is an international organization dedicated to fighting hunger,
poverty, and war. We believe that it is unconscionable that billions of dollars
are spent on murder and repression while billions of people go hungry. Rather
than wasting time, money, and energy on the futile task of asking politicans to
change their policies, Food Not Bombs directly creates a better world, starting
in our own communities.

1 in every 4 households in Canada is food insecure, with nearly a third of
children under 18 living in a food insecure household. Overwhelmingly affected
are black people, indigenous people living off-reserve, and other people of
colour.^1^ In Alberta, 57% of indigenous households on reserves are food
insecure.^2^ The cause is not laziness, stupidity, or lack of education.
Children miss meals every day because of systemic poverty and waste caused by
irresponsible government and corporate greed. Canada produces approximately 50
million tonnes of food waste each year, with 53% of waste coming from
corporations.^3^ While grocery prices have risen 5% over the past year and 27%
over the past 5 years, Loblaws chair Galen Weston made $2.55 billion in
2025.^4^ Food Not Bombs directly responds to this waste by redirecting
perfectly good food that would otherwise be thrown away, ensuring its quality,
and preparing and serving healthy meals to those most in need.

While nearly 10 million Canadians suffer from hunger, our federal government
allowed more than $14 million worth of direct arms transfers to Israel in
2025^5^ and has provided millions of dollars in subsidies to Canadian companies
supporting ICE operations in the United States.^6^ While over 3700 Edmontonians
struggle with homelessness,^7^ our police chief travels to Israel^8^ to study
the policing practices of an apartheid state.^9^ Food Not Bombs opposes these
wasteful expenditures and immoral actions which come at the expense of the most
vulnerable.

Food Not Bombs is not a charity. We do not believe that hunger is some eternal
problem that can only be addressed by the altruism of the rich and powerful.
Rather, hunger is caused by the rich and powerful. The hunger and want caused
by greed, waste, hate, and callousness can only be addressed by the people
themselves working together to achieve sustainable food sovereignty for
themselves. Food Not Bombs is on the front lines of this struggle, directly
recovering food that would otherwise go to waste and providing fresh, healthy,
vegan meals for anyone who is hungry.
