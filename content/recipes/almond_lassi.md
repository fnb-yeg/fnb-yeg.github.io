A traditional Indian yogurt drink made instead with almond milk. Ingredients:

- **2 Cups** Almond milk or almond beverage, chilled
- **3** Pitted dates
- **1 tsp** Ground cardamom
- A pinch of black pepper

Equipment:

- Blender

Place all ingredients in blender and blend until smooth, about 2 minutes.
Refrigerate at least 30 min before serving.
