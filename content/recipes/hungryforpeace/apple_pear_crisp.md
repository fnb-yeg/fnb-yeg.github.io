## For 6
Equipment:

- 3 12"x18" baking pans

### Filling
Ingredients:

- **5** apples *OR* **5** pears
- **1** lemon, juice of
- **1 cup** maple syrup/agave nectar (optional)
- **3 tbsp** vanilla
- **1 tbsp** cinnamon
- **1 tbsp** powdered ginger
- **1 tsp** nutmeg
- **1 tsp** allspice

Preheat oven to 350&deg;F. Core and slice the apples and pears. (Peeling is not
necessary.) In a mixing bowl, mix sliced fruit with remaining ingredients until
every piece of fruit is covered. Place into greased baking pans in an even
layer.

### Topping
Ingredients:

- **2 cups** rolled oats
- **2 cups** whole wheat flour
- **1 tbsp** cinnamon
- **1 tbsp** nutmeg
- **1 tbsp** allspice
- **2 tsp** ground cloves
- **1 tsp** sea salt (optional)
- **&frac14; cup** margarine
- **&frac14; cup** maple syrop or molasses or agave nectar (optional)
- **5 tbsp** vanilla

In a large mixing bowl, mix the oats, flour and spices. Break margarine into
small pieces and work into the dry mixture with your hands. Mix syrup and
vanilla together, then add to the topping and mix very well. Crumble the
topping over the fruit in the baking pans and bake in over at 350 degrees for
at least 1 hour, until the topping is golden brown, the fruit is soft, and
there is liquid on the bottom. Serve hot with vegan ice cream.

## For 100
Equipment:

- 3 12"x18" baking pans

### Filling
Ingredients:

- **40** apples
- **40** pears
- **10** lemons, juice of
- **5 cups** maple syrop/agave nectar (optional)
- **¼ cup** vanilla
- **½ cup** cinnamon
- **2 tbsp** powdered ginger
- **1 tbsp** nutmeg
- **1 tbsp** allspice

Preheat oven to 350&deg;F. Core and slice the apples and pears. (Peeling is not
necessary.) In a mixing bowl, mix sliced fruit with remaining ingredients until
every piece of fruit is covered. Place into greased baking pans in an even
layer.

### Topping
Ingredients:

- **20 cups** rolled oats
- **20 cups** whole wheat flour
- **&frac12; cup** cinnamon
- **2 tbsp** nutmeg
- **2 tbsp** allspice
- **1 tbsp** ground cloves
- **1 tbsp** sea salt (optional)
- **4 lbs** margarine
- **5 cups** maple syrup or agave nectar (optional)
- **½ cup** vanilla

In a large mixing bowl, mix the oats, flour and spices. Break margarine into
small pieces and work into the dry mixture with your hands. Mix syrup and
vanilla together, then add to the topping and mix very well. rumble the topping
over the fruit in the baking pans and bake in over at 350 degrees for at least
1 hour, until the topping is golden brown, the fruit is soft, and there is
liquid on the bottom. Serve hot with vegan ice cream.
