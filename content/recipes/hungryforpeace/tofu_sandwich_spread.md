## For 6
Ingredients:

- **¼ cup** miso
- **¼ cup** water
- **1 cup** tahini
- **2 lbs** crumbled tofu, soft can be the best
- **2** lemons, juice of

 Other Options:

- **2 tsp** cumin or coriander
- **2&ndash;3 cloves** garlic powder
- **1** small diced onion
- **2 stalks** diced celery
- **⅛ cup** Alaria, Dulse, Kelp, Nori or other seaweed

Equipment:

- mixing bowl

In one bowl, mix the miso and water into a smooth paste, then add tahini to the
mix (add additional water to make a smooth, creamy paste). Drain tofu of excess
water and crumble by hand into the very large bowl. Squeeze the lemon juice
over the tofu. Add miso/tahini mixture, and mix well. Add optional ingredients
if desired, and spread on your favorite bread with lettuce, sprouts, and tomato
slices.

### Vegan Tuna-like Tofu Spread
crush seaweed into the tofu spread to give it a tuna like taste. You can use
Maria, Dulse, Kelp, Nori or any edible seaweed you happen to find. Harvesting
seaweed yourself can also be rewarding.

## For 100
Ingredients:

- **3 cups** miso
- **3 cups** water
- **8 cups** tahini
- **25 lbs** crumbled tofu, firm can be best
- **25** lemons, juice of

 Other Options:

- **2 tbsp** cumin or coriander
- **½ cup** garlic powder
- **8 cups** diced onion
- **8 cups** diced celery
- **3 cups** Alaria, Dulse, Kelp, Nori or other seaweed

Equipment:

- 1 medium mixing bowl
- 1 very large mixing bowl

The process is the same for making tofu spread for a hundred as you would for
six people. In the medium bowl, mix the miso and water into a smooth paste,
then add tahini to the mix (add additional water to make smooth, creamy paste).
Drain tofu of excess water and crumble by hand into the very large bowl.
Squeeze the lemon juice over the tofu. Add miso/tahini mixture, and mix well.
Add optional ingredients if desired, and spread on your favorite bread with
lettuce, sprouts, and tomato slices.

### Vegan Tuna-like Tofu Spread
Crush seaweed into the tofu spread to give it a tuna like taste. You can use
Alaria, Dulse, Kelp, Nori or any edible seaweed you happen to find. Again
harvesting seaweed yourself can also be rewarding. It is important to stay
clear from areas where the seaweed could be contaminated with oil, radiation or
other toxins.
