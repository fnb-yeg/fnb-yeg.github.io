## For 6
Ingredients:

- **6 cups** grated carrots
- **8 cups** 'nayonnaise' (or make your own)
- **1 tbsp** sea salt
- **2 heads** shredded green cabbage
- **1 tbsp** lemon juice
- **2 pinches** black pepper

Equipment:

- large mixing bowl

To make coleslaw for 6 people you follow the same process as when making
coleslaw for 100 servings. Shred cabbage and grate carrots, then mix in a very
large mixing bowl and serve immediately. Serve cold.

You can make your own vegan mayonnaise by blending one cup of tofu, lemon
juice, and two teaspoons of garlic powder.
