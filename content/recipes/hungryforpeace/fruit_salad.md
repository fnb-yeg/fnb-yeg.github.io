## For 6
Ingredients:

- **10 pieces** assorted fruit (apples, oranges, pears, peaches, bananas, pineapples, berries, raisins and so on)
- **2** lemons juice of

Equipment:

- mixing bowl
- small serving bowl
- plastic storage buckets with lids

To make fruit salad for six you follow the same directions you would use to
make 100 servings. You will have much less compost when you make fruit salad
for six. There are many wonderful coconut based yogurts and other vegan
toppings you can add to your fresh fruit. Go wild!

## For 100
Ingredients:

- **100 pieces** assorted fruit (apples, oranges, pears, peaches, bananas, pineapples, berries, raisins and so on)
- **20** lemons juice of

Equipment:

- large mixing bowl
- plastic storage buckets with lids

Cut the fruit into bite-sized pieces. In a large mixing bowl, mix fruit
together with lemon juice, coating all pieces. The lemon juice helps slow the
browning which occurs when fruit is exposed to the air. Store fruit in plastic
"tofu" buckets with tight fitting lids and refrigerate, if possible. Serve in
small portions using a small serving bowl. This salad also tastes great with
granola, shredded coconut, or nondairy icecream.
