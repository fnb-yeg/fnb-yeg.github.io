Ingredients:

- **5 cups / 1.2kg** fruit
- **1 cup / 227g** soft tofu
- **1 cup / 250mL** water

This is the smoothy used in the Tofu Challenge. Take your discarded fruit and
cut out the bad parts and cut up the rest in small chunks. Add the fruit to
water and soft tofu in a blender or food processor. Blend until smooth. Add
more water to make it the best texture for drinking.
