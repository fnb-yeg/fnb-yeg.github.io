## For 6
Ingredients:

- **½ cup** tahini
- **2** lemons juice of
- **½ cup** nutritional yeast
- **1 tbsp** toasted sesame oil (optional)
- **2-3 cloves** of garlic
- **½ cups** water

Other Options:

- apple juice or cider

Equipment:

- blender or whisk

Again this is the same process used when making dressing or 100 salads. Place
half of all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth. Add more water or
lemon or apple juice as necessary, to make a thick, creamy dressing. Repeat.

## For 100
Ingredients:

- **5 cups** tahini
- **5** lemons juice of
- **1 cup** nutritional yeast
- **2 tbsp** toasted sesame oil (optional)
- **7-14 cloves** of garlic
- **2 cups** water

Other Options:

- apple juice or cider

Equipment:

- blender or whisk

Place half of all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth. Add more
water or lemon or apple juice as necessary, to make a thick, creamy dressing.
Repeat.
