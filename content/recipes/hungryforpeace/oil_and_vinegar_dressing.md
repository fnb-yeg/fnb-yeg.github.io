## For 6
Ingredients:

- **½ cup** olive oil
- **¼ cup** balsamic vinegar
- **1** lemons juice of
- **4 tsp** diced fresh garlic
- **2 tsp** thyme
- **2 tsp** basil
- **2 tsp** oregano
- **2 tsp** sea salt (optional)
- **2 tsp** black pepper
- **2 tsp** ginger powder

Equipment:

- 2 2L jars with lids

Just as you do when making dressing for 100 servings put half of all
ingredients in each jar and shake well. Shake again before every serving.
Variations include leaving out the oil, using only lemon juice and no vinegar;
using tamari instead of salt; adding nutritional yeast; adding apple or orange
juice, and so on. (Have fun making your dressing tasty!)

## For 100
Ingredients:

- **8 cups** olive oil
- **2 cups** balsamic vinegar
- **10** lemons juice of
- **4 tbsp** diced fresh garlic
- **2 tbsp** thyme
- **2 tbsp** basil
- **2 tbsp** oregano
- **2 tbsp** sea salt (optional)
- **2 tbsp** black pepper
- **2 tbsp** ginger powder

Equipment:

- 2 2L jars with lids

Put half of all ingredients in each jar and shake well. Shake again before
every serving. Variations include leaving out the oil, using only lemon juice
and no vinegar; using tamari instead of salt; adding nutritional yeast; adding
apple or orange juice, and so on. (Go ahead, be creative!)
