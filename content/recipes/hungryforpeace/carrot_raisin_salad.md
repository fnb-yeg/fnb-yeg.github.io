## For 6
Ingredients:

- **6** carrots, grated
- **1 cup** raisins
- **1 cups** "Nayonnaise" or make your own non-dairy mayonaise
- **1-2** lemons, juice of

Equipment:

- large mixing bowl

The process is the same as when making this salad for 100 servings. Grate
carrots, then mix all ingredients in a large mixing bowl. Serve cold. Note:
Nayonnaise is a brand of non-dairy mayonnaise made by Nayosa. It is an
excellent substitute for mayonnaise. However, you can alse make your own by
blending 1 cup of tofu with lemon juice, vinegar, and two teaspoons of garlic
poweder. You can add a touch of olive oil if you wish.
