## For 6

Ingredients:

- **2 cups** rolled oats
- **2 cups** barley flakes
- **¼ cups** almonds
- **¼ cups** shredded coconut
- **⅛ cups** sunflower seeds
- **⅛ cups** sesame seeds
- **¼ cups** olive oil (optional)
- **¼ cups** maple syrup, molasses or dark agave nectar
- **1 tbsp** vanilla
- **1 cups** raisins or chopped apples
- **¼ tsp** salt (optional)

Alternatives:

- wheat flakes
- rye flakes

Equipment:

- Large mixing bowl
- Medium saucepan
- 2 flat baking trays

Preheat oven to 300&deg;F (150&deg;C). Mix dry ingredients together in a large
bowl. In a saucepan, heat oil if using it, maple syrop and vanilla only until
warm enough to soak into the dry ingredients. Pour this mixture over the dry
ingredients and mix thoroughly, then spread into several flat baking trays. The
layer of granola should be no more than 1-inch thick. Toast in a 300&deg;F oven
for 15-20 minutes, stirring every few minutes. Granola is done when golden
brown. Mix in raisins at this point. When cool, serve granola with soy milk or
fruit juice and sliced fresh fruit.

## For 100

Ingredients:

- **40 cups** rolled oats
- **40 cups** barley flakes
- **10 cups** almonds
- **10 cups** shredded coconut
- **5 cups** sunflower seeds
- **2 cups** sesame seeds
- **6 cups** olive oil (optional)
- **5 cups** maple syrop, molasses or dark agave nectar
- **½ cup** vanilla
- **10 cups** raisins or chopped apples
- **1 tbsp** salt (optional)

Alternatives:

- wheat flakes
- rye flakes

Equipment:

- Large mixing bowl
- Medium saucepan
- Several flat baking trays

Preheat oven to 300&deg;F (150&deg;C). Mix dry ingredients together in a large
bowl. In a saucepan, heat oil if using it, maple syrup and vanilla only until
warm enough to soak into the dry ingredients. Pour this mixture over the dry
ingredients and mix thoroughly, then spread into several flat baking trays. The
layer of granola should be no more than 1-inch thick. Toast in a 300&deg;F oven
for 15-20 minutes, stirring every few minutes. Granola is done when golden
brown. Mix in raisins at this point. When cool, serve granola with soy milk or
fruit juice and sliced fresh fruit.
