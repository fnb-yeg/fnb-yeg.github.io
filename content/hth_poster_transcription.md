Food Not Bombs YEG Helping the Humans Update

* FNB YEG is running a program to provide free vegan homemade meals within 4 days of intake.
* You will be supplied with an intake form requesting name, phone number, address, number in household, and dietary restrictions.
* We will serve you up to 4 times
* If you need permanent supports we can help with connection to resources in the city
* Let us know if you need food of want to help out!
