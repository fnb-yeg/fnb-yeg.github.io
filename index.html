Food Not Bombs

Winter 2026 Newsletter

Future Organization Stability

Food Not Bombs has had some tumultuous times in its history, including recent history, but things are stable for now. We truly are coming into our own and meeting with a lot of success. We are in our third year of taking on practicum students from the U of A, our budget remains well balanced, and our reputation continues to improve.

Reaching Our Community and Changing Lives

Food Not Bombs recently took a closer look at the numbers behind our programs to better understand the impact we are making within our community. What we found highlights just how many individuals and families rely on these programs and the growing demand for accessible food and community support.

Through our Helping the Humans programming, we serve approximately 1500 people every year. Our Community Meals program also provides ongoing support, serving roughly 40 people each month. Additionally, our Birthday Club registration form fills up every time it opens, demonstrating the continued interest and need for the opportunities we provide.

Our Programs

Food Not Bombs offers a variety of community programming!

Our core programming includes:

  • Community garden (wrapped up for 2026, will resume in 2027)
  • Community pantry
  • Community meals and encampment outreach
  • Helping the Humans and Birthday Club
  • Fruit Collection (wrapped up for 2026, will resume in 2027)
  • Community Service Learning
