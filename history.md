FNB Edmonton was founded in 2018 by local activists from the Young Communist
League and the Anarchist community. It has grown since then from 5 founding
members to over 225. The first FNB meal was served at a rally held by Memoria
Viva, a left-wing Chilean organisation in Edmonton.

We have served dozens of meals to both political activists and unhoused people.
Some of our largest were the Climate Strike in 2019 where we served 300 people,
the meal on Feb. 15th, 2020, where we served 50 people and had 11 volunteers
helping between cooking and serving, and the Street Outreach YEG meal on July
17th, 2022, where we served 150 people and had 16 volunteers helping out. To
this day, FNB is thriving, sometimes having as many as 12 people attend our
monthly meetings, and a similar number helping out at the community meals
between cooking, serving, and driving.

In 2026, FNB YEG continues to serve meals once a month, usually to around 40
people. We maintain 6 programs, 3 of which are seasonal. FNB YEG is a member of
a global movement founded by Keith McHenry among others, in the 1980s. The
global FNB movement has dozens of autonomous chapters across nearly every
inhabited continent, all founded on the same core principles. FNB YEG has
had sporadic contact with other FNB chapters from the USA, the Phillipines,
Belarus, Russia, Poland, South Africa, and Palestine.
